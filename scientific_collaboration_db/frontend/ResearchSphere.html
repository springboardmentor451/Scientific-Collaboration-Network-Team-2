About the platform

About the Platform

The Scientific Collaboration Network Analyzer is a research collaboration management platform that centralizes information about researchers, institutions, publications, projects, conferences, citations, and collaborations.

It helps organizations manage and visualize research activities through a centralized system — from individual academic profiles to institution-wide reporting.

  • One system of record for research data across departments and institutions
  • Structured tracking of co-authorship and institutional partnerships
  • Descriptive, statistical reporting — no AI-based analysis
